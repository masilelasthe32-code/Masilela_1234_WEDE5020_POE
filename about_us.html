About Us


Shoprite is a South African multinational retail company that operates a chain of supermarkets and grocery stores across the African continent. It is one of the largest food retailers in Africa, with a strong presence in several countries. Shoprite offers a wide range of products, including groceries, fresh produce, household items, and more. The company is known for its commitment to providing affordable prices and quality products to its customers. Shoprite has become a household name in many African countries, serving millions of customers and contributing to the local economy through employment and community initiatives.

It was founded in 1979 by PEP Stores when a grocery company that had 8 stores were purchased from the Geller and Rogut families, these stores were in the Western Cape. Then in 1983 it has branched out to the Northern Cape which was the first store that was not in the Northern Cape. Then branched out to other provinces in the following years. Shoprite’s vision is to provide affordable goods and services to the public. Along with the uplifting the community by providing jobs and becoming Africa’s largest omnichannel retailer. Their target audience branches from low income to middle class households due to their stance of selling their goods at an affordable price. The Group’s cross-border expansion continues opening its first supermarket in Botswana. Money Markets begin in 1998 with a vision to offer customers comprehensive range of financial services and products tthrough conveniently located supermarket service counters. The first Medirite starts trading at Checkers Hyper in Brackenfell, allowing customers to fill their prescriptions while doing their grocery shopping. This opening denotes the broadening of the Group’s list of supplementary services. The Group enters Zimbabwe in September and opens a store in the Bulawayo Centre. The Group, however, divests from this country in September 2013. Entering Uganda in November it opens its first store, Shoprite Clock Tower, in Kampala. The Group discontinues its operations in Uganda in 2021. Freshmark becomes a division of the Group and is responsible for fruit and vegetable procurement, buying and distribution. This addition shows the Group’s further investment in supplementary services.
