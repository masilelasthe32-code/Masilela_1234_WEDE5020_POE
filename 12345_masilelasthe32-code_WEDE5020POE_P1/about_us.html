About Us


It was founded in 1979 by PEP Stores when a grocery company that had 8 stores were purchased from the Geller and Rogut families, these stores were in the Western Cape. Then in 1983 it has branched out to the Northern Cape which was the first store that was not in the Northern Cape. Then branched out to other provinces in the following years. Shoprite’s vision is to provide affordable goods and services to the public. Along with the uplifting the community by providing jobs and becoming Africa’s largest omnichannel retailer. Their target audience branches from low income to middle class households due to their stance of selling their goods at an affordable price.
